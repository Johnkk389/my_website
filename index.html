A hillside stay, at a human pace

Upslope Bnb sits on a quiet slope away from the noise of the city — a handful of rooms, a shared veranda, and breakfast that's cooked, not catered. Whether you're passing through or staying a while, the idea is the same: slow down, look at the hills, and let someone else make the coffee.

  • Get fresh ingredients for your meals, straight from the farm.
  • Get close up views of the Mountain from anywhere
  • Warm, hands-on hosting — we're usually around
